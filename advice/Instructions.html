Overview

You are participating in a decision-making experiment. You will report your beliefs about certain things. Your earnings will depend on the beliefs you report across four tasks: a weight estimation task, a height estimation task, an urns task, and a song ranking task. The weight and height tasks each contain one question; the urns and song ranking tasks each contain two questions, for a total of six questions. You will answer each question by allocating tokens across a set of possible answers. The possible answers are grouped in categories called "bins".

AT THE START OF THE SESSION, you will RANDOMLY be assigned to receive advice from one of three groups: Algorithmic Advice or Human Advice, or No Advice.

For each question you will complete two reporting rounds. Each question will start with round 1. After you have completed round 1, you may or may not purchase and receive advice. Then you can proceed to round 2. Some tasks feature multiple questions; in those cases, you will complete round 1 for all questions in that task before moving on to round 2.

Round 1 - Initial or Pre-Beliefs

For each question, you will give a report about what you believe to be the correct answer. You will use a slider interface as illustrated in the example below to allocate {{ num_tokens }} tokens across a set of possible outcomes in 10 bins. For this example, you would report your beliefs about what percentage of the Earth's surface is covered in water. The first bin corresponds to your belief that the percentage of the Earth's surface covered in water is between 0% to 10%, the second bin corresponds to your belief that the percentage of water covering is between 11%-20%, and so on. The 10th bin represents the outcome of 91%-100%. The tokens you allocate to each bin will reflect your beliefs about the percentage of the earth's surface is covered in water.

You must allocate all 100 tokens.

The more tokens you place on the correct outcome, the more points you earn. You will receive a cash payment based on the points you earn for reporting your beliefs. Each report pays between $0 and $50. For each report, your points determine your chance of earning $50. The more points you earn, the higher your chance of earning $50. Therefore, your best strategy to maximize your earnings is to always allocate your tokens honestly according to your true beliefs.

Allocation

To illustrate how you use these sliders, suppose you think there is a fair chance that the percentage of the Earth's surface that is covered in water is between 55%-75%. Then you might allocate the 100 tokens in the following way: 30 tokens to the interval to 51%-60%, 30 tokens to the interval 61%-70%, and 40 tokens to the 71%-80% interval. After you have made your selection, confirm your choices by clicking on the Submit button.

As you allocate tokens by using the ∧ and ∨ buttons to move tokens up or down, the number of points displayed above each bar will update automatically. These points determine your earnings if the bar selected corresponds to the correct outcome. If you earn 100 points, you will receive $50 with 100% certainty. But if you earn 0 points, you will receive $0 with certainty.

This display illustrates that if the percentage of the Earth's surface that is covered in water fell in the interval 71%-80%, you would earn 73 points. So, you would have a 73% chance of earning $50 rather than $0. You would earn less than 73 points for any other outcome. You would earn 63 points if the percentage of water covering is between 61% and 70%, You would also earn 63 points if the outcome is between 51% and 60%. Since the points are translated into cash payments, it is important to consider each question carefully and report your beliefs as accurately as possible.

Advice Phase

After completing Round 1, you may have the opportunity to purchase advice before making your final decision. You can only purchase advice if you were assigned to the Algorithmic or Human advice conditions. You will be shown a price list with varying costs for advice. You must make a choice in every row about whether you want to buy the advice at that specific price.

If you purchase advice, the cost will be deducted from your $10.00 advice fund, called an endowment. If you are in the No Advice group, you will simply move to round 2.

Round 2 - Final or Post-beliefs

After the advice phase, you will have the opportunity to update your beliefs. You will once again allocate {{ num_tokens }} tokens across 10 bins. You may change your token allocation based on the advice you received, or you may keep it exactly the same. Suppose after receiving advice you decided to put all of your eggs in one basket and allocate all 100 tokens to the 71% and 80% interval. Then you would have the earnings outcome shown below.

Note the “good news” and “bad news” here. If the percentage of the Earth's surface covered in water is indeed between 71% and 80%, you earn the maximum payoff, which is $50. But if the true percentage of water covering is 66%, then would have earned nothing in this task. It is up to you to balance the strength of your personal beliefs with the possibility of them being wrong. After you have completed all the tasks, the correct answers will be shown on the final screen.

Payment

Your total payment consists of three parts:

  • Participation Fee: You have already earned a $10 show-up fee for agreeing to participate in this experiment, which will be paid in cash at the end of the session.
  • Endowment: You are given a ${{ endowment }} endowment to cover the cost of purchasing advice across all 4 tasks. Any money left over from your endowment at the end of the experiment is yours to keep.
  • Task Earnings: At the end of the experiment, the ground truth will be compared to your token allocations to determine accuracy. Based on your score, you can earn up to $50 per report. Remember, however, that only ONE report will randomly be selected for payment.
